Privacy policy

KEF Control for Windows · last updated 15 November 2026

KEF Control is a free, open-source app that controls KEF wireless speakers from a Windows PC. It's built to collect nothing about you.

What the app does with data

  • Your speakers: the app talks directly to your KEF speakers on your local network to read and change their settings (volume, input, sound settings and so on). This traffic stays on your network.
  • Settings on your PC: the speakers you've added (name, IP and MAC address), your sound profiles and app options are saved in %APPDATA%\KEFControl\config.json on your computer only.
  • Update check: if "Check for updates automatically" is on, the app asks GitHub (api.github.com) once a day for the latest version number. The request contains no personal data. GitHub may log the request as described in GitHub's privacy statement. You can turn this off in Settings → App. The Microsoft Store version doesn't make this request, because the Store handles updates.
  • Diagnostics: "Copy diagnostics" copies technical information (app version, Windows version, speaker model and firmware, recent errors) to your clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to paste it into a bug report.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking or ads.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.
